## Scheme of assessment

| Paper | Name | Duration | Marks | Weighting |
|-------|------|----------|-------|-----------|
| 1 (6091/01) | Multiple Choice | 1 h | 40 | 30% |
| 2 (6091/02) | Structured and Free Response | 1 h 45 min | 80 | 50% |
| 3 (6091/03) | Practical Test | 1 h 50 min | 40 | 20% |

**Paper 1** — 40 compulsory four-option multiple-choice items.

**Paper 2**
- Section A (70 marks, compulsory): variable number of structured questions. The
  last two questions carry 20 marks between them, one of which is a **data-based
  question worth 8–12 marks** built on a stem of information.
- Section B (10 marks): two free-response questions, candidates answer **one**.

**Paper 3** — two sections, 55 minutes with apparatus per section.

## Assessment objectives

| AO | Title | Weighting |
|----|-------|-----------|
| AO1 | Knowledge with understanding | ~45% of theory marks (~15% simple recall) |
| AO2 | Handling information and solving problems | ~55% of theory marks |
| AO3 | Experimental skills and investigations | Paper 3 only |

AO3 skill areas: **P** (Planning, ~15% of Paper 3), **MMO** (Manipulation,
measurement and observation), **PDO** (Presentation of data and observations),
**ACE** (Analysis, conclusions and evaluation) — MMO/PDO/ACE ≈ 85% of Paper 3.

## Notable changes in the 2024 revision (vs. 2023)

These matter for question generation because a bank built from older Ten-Year-Series
papers will contain out-of-syllabus items:

- **Temperature** is no longer a standalone topic (thermometers, calibration removed).
- **Gas laws** (quantitative) removed from the kinetic particle model topic.
- **Sound** is no longer a standalone topic — folded into *General Properties of Waves*.
- Reading of **vernier calipers / micrometer screw gauge** removed.
- **Energy resources** moved into the *Energy* topic.
- Energy is reframed around **energy stores** (kinetic, internal, potential) and
  transfers, rather than "forms of energy".
- General shift from recall towards application and conceptual reasoning.
